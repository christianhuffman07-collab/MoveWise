This is a financial timing model, not a forecast. It compares estimated ending home equity, unused sale cash, housing costs, commute costs, transaction costs, appreciation and mortgage paydown. It does not price lifestyle benefits, job risk, taxes on a sale, or the value of time saved commuting.
ESTIMATE ONLY — does not include PMI or taxes due on a sale. The move-timing section separately models buyer closing costs, moving costs and a user-entered future mortgage-rate assumption. Property tax and insurance are averaged monthly (escrow-style). Commute cost can use a flat per-mile rate, gas + MPG, or the selected vehicle estimate. Location-based property tax uses public Census data when available. Consult a lender for an official quote.
